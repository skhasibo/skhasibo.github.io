I am a doctoral researcher in Computational Chemistry and Biophysics at the **Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science (IACS)**, Kolkata, working under the supervision of **[Prof. Avisek Das](https://avisekdaslab.com/)**.

My research leverages high-performance computing to investigate complex biological systems through molecular simulations and multiscale computational approaches. I utilize all-atom molecular dynamics simulations, quantum mechanics/molecular mechanics (QM/MM) hybrid methods, and density functional theory (DFT) to study biomolecular structure, conformational dynamics, and molecular mechanisms. I also develop scientific Python workflows and apply statistical and machine learning methods for molecular simulation data analysis.

### **Latest Updates**

* **[Jul 2026]** Released two open-source GitHub repositories demonstrating supervised machine learning (Random Forest and XGBoost) and deep representation learning (PCA, Autoencoder, and Variational Autoencoder) workflows for molecular dynamics feature analysis.

* **[Feb 2026]** Our preprint, **"Cysteine Protonation States in Copper Trafficking Proteins and Its Influence on Biological Cu(I) Transfer Mechanism,"** is available on [ChemRxiv](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.10001925/v3).

* **[May 2025]** Presented research on the ATP7B copper transport mechanism at the **Gordon Research Conference (GRC): Mechanisms of Membrane Transport**, Les Diablerets, Switzerland.

* **[2024]** Co-first author publication on the role of explicit solvation in Cu(I) transfer published in [*The Journal of Physical Chemistry B*](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpcb.3c07327).
