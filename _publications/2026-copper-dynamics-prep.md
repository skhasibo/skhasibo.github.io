This collaborative work focuses on the initial stages of copper transfer in protein-protein encounter complexes. 

This study establishes a mechanistic framework for Cu(I) transfer between holo-chaperones and the metal-binding domain (MBD). By integrating molecular dynamics, machine learning–based ensemble analysis, and QM/MM validation, the work demonstrates that productive binding-site preorganization emerges from conformational variability and entropy–enthalpy balance rather than purely electrostatic stabilization.
