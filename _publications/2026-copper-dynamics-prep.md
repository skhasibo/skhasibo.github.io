This collaborative work investigates the early stages of Cu(I) transfer in transient protein–protein encounter complexes between metallochaperones and metal-binding domains.

By integrating long-timescale molecular dynamics simulations, free-energy landscape analysis, and QM/MM characterization of reactive conformational states, the study explores how conformational heterogeneity and electrostatic interactions influence the formation of productive Cu(I)-transfer configurations.
