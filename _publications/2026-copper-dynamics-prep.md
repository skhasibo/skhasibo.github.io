This collaborative work focuses on the initial stages of copper transfer in protein-protein encounter complexes. 

By applying multidimensional machine learning algorithms and multiscale QM/MM calculations, we demonstrate that the early stages of copper transfer are not entirely driven by electrostatic control. Instead, the process relies on a delicate balance of dynamical variability and entropy-enthalpy compensation to successfully form reaction intermediates.
